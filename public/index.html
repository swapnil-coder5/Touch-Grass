    OPEN AT THE ROOTS

    Little footprint.
    Wide-open possibilities.

    Touch Grass uses wink-nlp and its open-source English model to tokenize your words, find their base forms, and identify parts of speech. Those model outputs feed a transparent activity ranking system.

    There’s no cloud AI subscription and no third-party AI request in the default flow. Run it on your own laptop; after installing dependencies, it works without internet.

    Open innovation lets you inspect the ranking, change the activities, or add an open-weight model with Ollama. Your mood and reflections don’t need to become someone else’s data.

    This is a local NLP planner, not a generative language model. Reflections are stored only in your browser. On a hosted server, your planning request is processed by that server.
